# Event-linked raid and Max Battle schedules (8 November 2026)

The main **Into the Wild** and **GO Wild Area Global** entries remain in the Events row. Separate battle entries have `category: "raid"` or `category: "max-battle-rotation"`, a `linkedEventId` to the parent event, and `featuredPokemon` images.

- Into the Wild: 5-star Crowned Sword Zacian and Crowned Shield Zamazenta; 3-star Druddigon and Orthworm; Zacian Monday and Zamazenta Tuesday 6–7 p.m. raid hours. Featured Max Battles include Ralts, Machop, and scheduled Registeel / Regidrago / Latias & Latios.
- GO Wild Area Global: Saturday 5-star Zekrom and White Kyurem; Sunday 5-star Reshiram and Black Kyurem; costumed Machamp 3-star both days. Saturday Dynamax Dialga / Gigantamax Duraludon and Sunday Dynamax Palkia / Gigantamax Duraludon Max Battles.
- Time windows are local to the player. All original event posters and other records are preserved.
- Sources: https://pokemongo.com/news/into-the-wild-2026 and https://pokemongo.com/gowildarea/global

These added battle-group cards do not represent independent new festivals, and the underlying main event is not duplicated. Clone Special Raid Day remains excluded.
